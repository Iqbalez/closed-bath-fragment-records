# Change log

## 3.0.0

Profile and final bath measurement noise is reduced from standard deviation 0.008 to 0.004; diffusivity assay noise, equations, distributions, sampling and labels are unchanged. With the higher noise, competent solvers reached the same observation-noise limit and could not be separated. Records generated by 3.0.0 are incompatible with 2.0.0 records; frozen splits must be rebuilt from new data.

## 2.0.0

Original coupled finite-bath model with separate known initial slab concentrations, known feed/flow/volume, and final-only bath/profile observations. Conservative24-cell/240-step transport replaces the independent prescribed-boundary formulation. Ground truth remains eight distinct opening ranks. All numerical assumptions and synthetic limitations are disclosed. This source is incompatible with1.0.0 raw records; frozen splits must be rebuilt from new data.
